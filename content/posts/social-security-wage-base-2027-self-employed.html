The short version

  • $192,300 in 2027: the most earnings that Social Security tax applies to, up from $184,500 in 2026.
  • Self-employed: you pay 12.4% Social Security tax up to the cap and 2.9% Medicare tax on everything, on 92.35% of your net profit.
  • Up to $967.20 more: the most the higher cap adds to one person's 2027 Social Security tax ($7,800 × 12.4%).
  • Who feels it: net profit above about $199,800, or a W-2 job plus a business that together pass $184,500.

Bottom line: If your earnings were near or above the cap in 2026, add up to $967.20 to your 2027 tax estimate. Everyone below the cap pays the same rate as before.

The 2027 Social Security wage base is $192,300. The wage base is the yearly cap on earnings that Social Security tax applies to. The Social Security Administration announced it in October 2026 along with a 3.6% benefit increase, and it takes effect on Jan. 1, 2027. Employees see it on their paychecks. If you work for yourself, it shows up in your self-employment tax on Schedule SE.

How self-employment tax works with the wage base

Self-employment tax is the Social Security and Medicare tax people who work for themselves pay, covering both the employee and employer shares. You figure it on Schedule SE:

  1. Take your net profit (Schedule C, or your share from a partnership).
  2. Multiply by 92.35%. That's your net earnings from self-employment.
  3. Pay 12.4% Social Security tax on those earnings, up to $192,300 in 2027.
  4. Pay 2.9% Medicare tax on all of them. There's no cap. An extra 0.9% Additional Medicare Tax applies to earnings over $200,000 single or $250,000 married filing jointly.

You can deduct half of your self-employment tax on Form 1040, so the higher cap also raises that deduction a little. You owe self-employment tax once net earnings reach $400.

The W-2 plus side business case

If you have a job and a business, Social Security tax on your wages counts first. Your employer withholds it on your $150,000 salary, and only the room left under the cap applies to your business: $34,500 in 2026, $42,300 in 2027. People in this spot hit the cap sooner than they expect, and they also pay a bit more when the cap rises. Schedule SE does this math for you, but your estimated payments should reflect it.

What to do now

  • Update your 2027 estimates. The first 2027 payment is due April 15, 2027. Our quarterly estimated tax calculator and 1099 tax calculator show the effect.
  • If you run an S corp: Social Security tax applies to the salary you pay yourself, not to distributions, so the cap matters only if your salary is near it. Quick answers

What is the maximum Social Security tax in 2027?

For a self-employed person, 12.4% of $192,300, or $23,845.20. An employee pays half of that, 6.2% or $11,922.60, and the employer pays the other half.

Does the Social Security wage base apply to Medicare?

No. Medicare tax (2.9% for the self-employed) applies to all net earnings, with an extra 0.9% above $200,000 single or $250,000 married filing jointly.

When does the 2027 wage base start?

Jan. 1, 2027. It applies to 2027 earnings, which you report on your 2027 return in 2028.

I have two W-2 jobs and paid too much Social Security tax. What happens?

If two or more employers together withheld Social Security tax on more than the wage base, you claim the excess as a credit on your Form 1040.

How much do I need to earn for a Social Security credit in 2027?

$1,970 of earnings per credit, up to four credits a year ($7,880 for all four).
