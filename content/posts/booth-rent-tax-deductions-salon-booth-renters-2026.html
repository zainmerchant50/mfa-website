Is booth rent tax deductible?

Yes. IRS Publication 334 says you can deduct rent as a business expense when it's for property you use in your business. A chair, booth or suite you rent to serve your own clients fits that rule.

Paying weekly by app, check or cash doesn't change that. Keep the lease and a payment history you can match to your bank account.

One timing rule: rent you pay in advance is deducted in the year it applies to. January 2027 rent paid in December 2026 is a 2027 deduction.

How much self-employment tax does a booth renter pay?

Self-employment tax for booth renters is 15.3%: 12.4% for Social Security and 2.9% for Medicare. You owe it once your net earnings from self-employment are $400 or more.

It's figured on your profit, not your sales. That's why booth rent and supplies matter twice: they cut income tax and self-employment tax.

For 2026, the Social Security part applies to the first $184,500 of combined wages, tips and net earnings. You also deduct the employer-equivalent portion (half) of the tax when you figure your adjusted gross income.

Do booth renters have to report cash and tips?

Yes. The IRS says all income is taxable unless the law says otherwise, even without a Form 1099-K. Card payments can trigger a 1099-K at any amount. Payment apps send one only over $20,000 and more than 200 transactions.

Tips are income too. The new tips deduction runs from 2025 through 2028, up to $25,000 a year, and phases out above $150,000 of modified AGI ($300,000 joint). The IRS tipped-jobs list includes barbers, hairstylists and cosmetologists (603), manicurists (605), eyebrow and eyelash techs (606) and makeup artists (607).

For self-employed pros, the deduction can't exceed net income from the business. Self-employed people in a "specified service trade or business" can't claim it, so check before you count on it. Try our no tax on tips calculator.

When are 2026 estimated taxes due?

Nobody withholds tax from booth rent income, so most booth renters pay quarterly with Form 1040-ES. The 2026 dates are April 15, June 15 and September 15, 2026, and January 15, 2027. You can skip the January payment if you file your 2026 return by February 1, 2027 and pay the full balance. Plan amounts with our quarterly estimated tax calculator.

Booth renter taxes in NJ and NY

  • New Jersey: net profits from business go on line 18 of Form NJ-1040 through Schedule NJ-BUS-1, starting from your federal Schedule C. A business loss gets no entry on line 18, so it doesn't reduce other New Jersey income.
  • New York: New York adjusted gross income starts from your federal adjusted gross income, so your Schedule C deductions carry into Form IT-201.
  • New York City: the 2025 NYC-202 instructions require an unincorporated business tax return when gross income from all business is more than $95,000. The rate is 4% after a $5,000 exemption.

FAQs

Can I deduct booth rent I pay in cash?

Yes, if it's rent for space you use in your business. Get a receipt or a written agreement for each payment so you can prove it.

Do I need an LLC to deduct booth rent?

No. A sole proprietor reports booth rent and other expenses on Schedule C with Form 1040. An LLC changes legal liability, not whether the rent is deductible.

Can a self-employed stylist claim no tax on tips?

Possibly. Hairstylists are on the IRS tipped-jobs list, but the deduction is limited to net business income, and specified service businesses are excluded. Check your situation before you file.
