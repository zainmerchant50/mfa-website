Writing off equipment:
Section 179 vs. bonus

Both let you deduct the full cost in year one. Here's how they differ.

Section 179

$2,560,000
2026 limit · phase-out above $4,090,000
  • Choose asset by asset, any amount up to cost
  • Can't exceed business taxable income (excess carries forward)
  • More than 50% business use required
  • Heavy SUVs (over 6,000 lbs.) capped at $32,000

Bonus depreciation

100%
Permanent · acquired after Jan. 19, 2025
  • No dollar cap
  • No business-income limit; can create a loss
  • New or used (if new to you)
  • Applies by asset class unless you elect out

Before Dec. 31 checklist

  1. Place it in service: set up and ready to use, not just ordered
  2. Log business use for vehicles from day one
  3. Save invoice, contract date and in-service date
  4. Book it as a fixed asset, not an expense
  5. Items ≤ $2,500: use the de minimis safe harbor
