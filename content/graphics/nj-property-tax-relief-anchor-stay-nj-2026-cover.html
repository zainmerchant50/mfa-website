New Jersey property tax relief:
one form, three programs

The PAS-1 application covers ANCHOR, Stay NJ and Senior Freeze for 2025.

ANCHOROwners & renters
$1,500Homeowners, top amount
  • Homeowners: $1,500 if income is $150,000 or less, $1,000 up to $250,000
  • Renters: $450, or $700 if 65+ (income up to $150,000)
  • Age 65+ by Dec. 31, 2025 adds $250
  • Your main home on Oct. 1, 2025
Stay NJHomeowners 65+
$6,500Maximum benefit
  • $6,500 if income is $100,000 or less
  • $5,000 up to $150,000; $4,000 up to $200,000
  • Owned and lived in the home all 12 months of 2025
  • Paid in installments, Feb. and May 2027
Senior Freeze65+ or disabled
$172,4752025 income limit
  • Reimburses increases in your property tax
  • Income limits: $168,268 (2024) and $172,475 (2025)
  • Owned and lived in the home since Dec. 31, 2022
  • 65+ or on Social Security disability by Dec. 31, 2025
File the PAS-1 yourself if you're a homeowner 65+ or receive Social Security or Railroad Retirement disability. Most homeowners under 65 and most renters were filed automatically (letters mailed Aug. 10, 2026). Apply at propertytaxreliefapp.nj.gov.
