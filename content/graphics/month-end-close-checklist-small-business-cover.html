Month-end close checklist
8 steps for small business

Run it after each month ends. Same order every time.

1Record everythingSales, deposits, bills and receipts for the month.
2Categorize the feedClear “uncategorized” and split mixed charges.
3ReconcileEvery bank and card account to the statement.
4Review A/R and A/PChase overdue invoices; enter unpaid bills.
5AdjustAccruals, prepaid costs used up, depreciation.
6Tie out taxesPayroll and sales tax liabilities match the returns.
7Review reportsP&L and balance sheet vs. last month.
8Lock the monthQuickBooks Online: set the closing date.
