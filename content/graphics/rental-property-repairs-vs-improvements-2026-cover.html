Repair or improvement?

Ask three questions about the work you paid for on your rental.

1Does it better it?Fixes a defect that was there when you bought it, enlarges it, or adds capacity or quality.
2Does it restore it?Replaces a major component or substantial structural part, or rebuilds after a casualty.
3Does it adapt it?Converts the property to a different use than when you started renting it.
Yes to any: improvementDepreciate over 27.5 years (residential rental), from the month it's placed in service. Unless a safe harbor below applies.
No to all: repairDeduct on Schedule E in the year you pay it. Cash basis: pay by Dec. 31, 2026 for a 2026 deduction.
