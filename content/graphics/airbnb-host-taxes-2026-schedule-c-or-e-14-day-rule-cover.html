Schedule C or E? Count your nights.

Three questions that decide how an Airbnb or Vrbo host reports for 2026.

Step 1
Your home, rented under 15 days?
Yes: don't report the rent or deduct rental expenses.
No: report it. Go to step 2.
Step 2
Hotel-style services during stays?
Yes (regular cleaning, fresh linen, maid service): Schedule C, possible 15.3% SE tax.
No (space only): Schedule E.
Step 3
Personal use over the limit?
Limit: greater of 14 days or 10% of fair-rental days.
Over it: split costs by days; the rental can't show a loss.
