AI in accounting and tax:
where it helps, where it stops

Use AI for the busywork. Keep a person on anything that ends up on a return.

Good uses of AIFaster
  • Sorting transactions into categories for you to review
  • Flagging issues like duplicates and reconciliation gaps
  • Chasing receipts and missing details
  • Drafting summaries, checklists and first-pass explanations
Keep a human onYour call
  • Anything you sign. You're responsible for your return, whoever prepares it
  • Complex tax questions. Verify every AI answer and number
  • Tax positions and advice that need judgment
  • Your data. Keep SSNs, bank and tax details out of public chatbots
