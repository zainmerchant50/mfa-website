The 2027 tax calendar

Key federal deadlines for individuals and small businesses (calendar-year filers).

Jan – Mar
  • Jan 15
    Q4 2026 estimated tax
  • Feb 1
    W-2s, 1099-NEC, most 1099s, Form 941 (Q4)
  • Feb 16
    1099-B and 1099-S to recipients
  • Mar 1
    Paper 1099s to the IRS
  • Mar 15
    Partnership (1065) and S corp (1120-S) returns, K-1s
  • Mar 31
    E-filed 1099s to the IRS
Apr – Jun
  • Apr 15
    Tax Day: Form 1040, Form 1120, extensions, Q1 2027 estimate
  • Apr 30
    Form 941 (Q1)
  • Jun 15
    Q2 2027 estimated tax; filers living abroad
Jul – Sep
  • Aug 2
    Form 941 (Q2)
  • Sep 15
    Q3 2027 estimated tax; extended 1065 and 1120-S
Oct – Jan
  • Oct 15
    Extended 2026 Form 1040 and 1120
  • Nov 1
    Form 941 (Q3)
  • Dec 15
    Q4 corporate estimate
  • Jan 18, '28
    Q4 2027 estimated tax

New Jersey

NJ-1040 due Apr 15 (extended Oct 15). Estimates follow the federal dates. ST-50 sales tax: Jan 20, Apr 20, Jul 20, Oct 20.

New York

IT-201 due Apr 15 (extended Oct 15). PTET election and return: Mar 15. Sales tax: Mar 22, Jun 21, Sep 20, Dec 20.
