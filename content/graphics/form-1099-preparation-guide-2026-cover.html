Do I need to issue
a Form 1099?

Run every payee through these four questions before January.

1
Paid by check, ACH, wire, cash, Zelle or a personal P2P transfer?Card, PayPal Goods & Services, Stripe, Square and business Venmo payments are excluded.
NOThe processor reports it on Form 1099-K. Don't issue a 1099-NEC/MISC.
2
Payee is an individual, sole prop, partnership or LLC (not taxed as a corporation)?Check the tax classification on their Form W-9.
NOCorporations generally don't get one, except attorneys and medical providers.
3
Paid for business services, rent, prizes or other income?Not merchandise, freight, or personal payments.
NOGoods and personal payments aren't reportable.
4
Total for the year is $2,000 or more?Calendar-year total per payee, all accounts combined. ($600 for 2025 payments.)
NOBelow the threshold, unless tax was withheld.
Issue a 1099-NEC or 1099-MISCCollect the W-9 first, and file on time.
