Small business write-offs:
what counts, what's limited

The 2026 numbers and the proof to keep, on one page.

Home office
$1,500

Simplified max: $5 per sq. ft. up to 300 sq. ft. Regular and exclusive use only.

Car, per mile
72.5¢ / 76¢

Jan. 1 to June 30 / July 1 to Dec. 31. Never the commute.

Business meals
50%

Entertainment 0%. Staff meals at the office 0% from 2026.

Client gifts
$25

Per recipient, per year. Wrapping and shipping don't count toward it.

Proof to keep

  1. Receipt or invoice: payee, date, what you bought
  2. Proof of payment: bank or card record
  3. Business purpose: who, why, for meals and gifts
  4. Mileage log kept as you go
  5. Asset file: cost, date in service, use
  6. Form W-9 from each contractor

How long to keep records

Most income and expense records3 years after filing
Payroll records4+ years
Unreported income over 25% / bad debts6 / 7 years
Equipment and propertyUntil 3 years after the return for the year you sell
