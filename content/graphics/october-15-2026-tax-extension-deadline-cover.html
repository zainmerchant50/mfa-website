Before Oct. 15 checklist

  • E-file your 2025 return. IRS Free File runs through Oct. 15 (AGI $89,000 or less).
  • Pay what you can now with IRS Direct Pay or your IRS Online Account.
  • Can't pay it all? File anyway, then request an IRS payment plan.
  • Self-employed? 2025 SEP-IRA and solo 401(k) employer contributions are due by your extended due date.
  • New Jersey filers: NJ-1040 is also due Oct. 15 if 80% of your tax was paid by Apr. 15.
  • Keep copies of your return and records for at least 3 years.
