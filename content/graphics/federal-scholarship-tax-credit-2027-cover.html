Participating states for 2027 (IRS list, Sept. 14, 2026)

Does my gift qualify?

  • Made on or after Jan. 1, 2027. 2026 gifts don't count
  • Cash only: check, card or transfer. Not stock or property
  • To a listed SGO (scholarship granting organization) in a participating state
  • Not also deducted as a charitable gift, and reduced by any state credit for the same gift
  • Keep the SGO's acknowledgment for your 2027 return (filed in 2028)

New Jersey taxpayers

NJ status: not a participating state as of Sept. 14, 2026.

You can still give to an SGO in any participating state and claim the federal credit.

Your gift funds scholarships in that SGO's state.

Check the IRS list before you give. States can join later.

Credit vs. deduction: a $1,700 credit cuts your tax by $1,700. The same gift deducted in the 24% bracket saves about $408.
