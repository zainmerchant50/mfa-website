Filing taxes late:
the numbers and the next steps.

Missed the Oct. 15 extension deadline, or never filed for 2025? Start here.

What the IRS charges
5%
Late filing

Of the unpaid tax, each month or part of a month. Max 25%.

0.5%
Late payment

Of the unpaid tax, each month. Max 25%. Keeps running until paid.

$525
Minimum penalty

Once more than 60 days late, or 100% of the tax owed if less.

7%
Interest

A year, compounded daily (Oct.–Dec. 2026). Charged on penalties too.

The dates that matter
Apr. 15, 2026

Original due date. Payment was due, even with an extension.

Oct. 15, 2026

Extended due date. Late-filing penalty starts after this.

Dec. 14, 2026

Extension filers: file by today to avoid the $525 minimum.

Apr. 2029

3-year window to claim a 2025 refund closes.

Do this now

  1. File the return, even if you can't pay. It stops the 5% a month.
  2. Pay what you can today to shrink penalties and interest.
  3. Payment plan for the rest: apply online at irs.gov.
  4. Clean 3-year history? Automatic penalty relief may apply (2025 returns on).
  5. Owed a refund? Usually no late-filing penalty, but file before it expires.
  6. New Jersey too: 5% a month, up to 25%, plus interest.
The late-filing penalty is 10 times the late-payment penalty. If you can only do one thing this week, file.
