How long to keep
business records

Keep anything behind a return until the window to question that return closes.

3 yrsIRS defaultFrom the date you file. Early returns count as filed on the due date.
4 yrsPayroll recordsAfter the tax is due or paid, whichever is later.
6 yrsIncome left offIf over 25% of the gross income shown on the return.
7 yrsBad debtOr a worthless securities deduction.
