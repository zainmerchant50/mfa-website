1. Freelancers and 1099 contractors (self-employment tax, quarterly estimates, write-offs).
2. Single-member LLC and S corp owners; people forming an LLC ("[state] llc cost" searches).
3. Online sellers and creatives (Etsy, Shopify, artists): 1099-K, sales tax, bookkeeping.
4. International students and visa holders (F-1/OPT/H-1B) needing correct nonresident/resident returns.
5. NJ/NY households (ANCHOR, SALT, property-tax relief) as top-of-funnel content readers.
Pinterest audience skews female: side-hustle, small-business-mom, Etsy-seller and personal-finance boards.
