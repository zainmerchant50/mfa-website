What are the key tax deadlines in 2027?

The big ones: January 15 (last 2026 estimated payment), February 1 (W-2s and 1099-NECs), March 15 (partnership and S corp returns, and the S corp election for 2027), April 15 (individual and C corp returns, plus the first 2027 estimated payment), June 15 and September 15 (2027 estimates), and October 15 (extended individual and C corp returns). This calendar lists 54 federal, New Jersey and New York dates in all, 25 of them for 2026 returns and payments.

How to use this calendar

  • Filter by who files (self-employed, partnership, S corp, C corp, employer, nonprofit, sales tax seller), by federal, NJ or NY, and by tax year.
  • Add to your calendar one date or everything you see, with a 7-day reminder.
  • Weekends and holidays are already applied: a date that falls on a weekend or legal holiday moves to the next business day (for example, January 18, 2028 for the last 2027 estimate).
  • Disaster relief can push a date back. Check the source link before relying on it.
