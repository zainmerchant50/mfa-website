Your depreciation schedule

Straight line over 27.5 years with the mid-month convention (Publication 527). IRS tables round each year to three decimals, so your return can differ by a few dollars.

YearDepreciation

How is rental property depreciation calculated?

Divide the building's basis by 27.5 to get each full year's depreciation. Basis is what you paid plus certain closing costs and improvements, minus the value of the land. The first and last years are partial: the mid-month convention treats the property as placed in service in the middle of its first month.

  • Land is never depreciated. Split the price between land and building, for example with your property tax assessment.
  • It starts when the rental is ready and available, even if the first tenant arrives later.
  • Improvements are depreciated, repairs are deducted. A new roof or addition starts its own 27.5-year schedule. Read repairs vs improvements.
  • Allowed or allowable. When you sell, the IRS treats depreciation you could have taken as taken, so claim it every year.
