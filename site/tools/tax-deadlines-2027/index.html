How to use this calendar

Pick the entity you file as. The list shows federal, New Jersey and New York dates for calendar-year filers, in date order. Add one date to your calendar, or add everything on the list in one file. Dates that fall on a weekend or holiday move to the next business day, and the IRS or a state can change a date when it announces disaster relief, so check the source link before you rely on an extension.

  • Estimated tax. Use the quarterly estimated tax calculator to work out each payment.
