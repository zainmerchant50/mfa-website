How much of my tips can I deduct?

Up to $25,000 of qualified tips a year, for 2025 through 2028. The deduction drops by $100 for every full $1,000 of modified AGI over $150,000 ($300,000 if married filing jointly). You can take it whether or not you itemize. Married couples must file jointly, and you need a valid Social Security number.

Who counts as a tipped worker?

  • Your job must be on the IRS list of occupations that customarily received tips before 2025. Barbers, hairstylists, cosmetologists, nail techs, lash and brow techs and makeup artists are on it.
  • Employees: tips on your W-2 (box 7) and tips you reported on Form 4137 count.
  • Self-employed and booth renters: tips count too, but only up to the net profit of the business the tips came from. Some self-employed service businesses are excluded.
  • Tips must be voluntary. A required service charge is not a tip.

What the deduction does not do

It lowers your federal income tax only. Social Security and Medicare tax (self-employment tax if you rent a booth) still apply to every tip, and state rules can differ. Booth renter? See booth renter taxes for the full picture.
