Tax answers · S corp and LLC owners

When is the 2026 S corp return (Form 1120-S) due?

Short answer first, checked against official sources on November 17, 2026.

Short answer

March 15, 2027 for a calendar-year S corporation, together with a Schedule K-1 for each shareholder. Form 7004 extends the filing deadline six months, to September 15, 2027. The S corp itself usually pays no federal income tax; owners report their share of profit on their own returns.

Key facts

ReturnForm 1120-S plus Schedules K-1
DueMarch 15, 2027
ExtensionForm 7004, to September 15, 2027
Owners reportK-1 income on their personal returns
Partnerships (Form 1065)Same dates

What else to know

Owners can't file their personal return until they have their K-1, so filing the 1120-S on time keeps everyone's April deadline realistic. New Jersey and New York have their own S corp filings.
