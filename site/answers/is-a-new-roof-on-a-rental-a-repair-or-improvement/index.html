Is a new roof on a rental a repair or improvement?

Short answer first, checked against IRS sources on November 17, 2026.

Short answer

A new roof is an improvement. You add it to the property's basis and depreciate it over 27.5 years. Fixing a leak or replacing a few shingles is a repair, deducted in the year you pay. Items costing $2,500 or less per invoice or item can be deducted under the de minimis safe harbor if you elect it.

Key facts

Whole new roofImprovement, 27.5 years
Patch or a few shinglesRepair, deduct now
De minimis safe harbor$2,500 per invoice or item, elected each year
SourceIRS Publication 527

What else to know

The test is whether the work betters, restores or adapts the property. Read the full guide: rental property repairs vs improvements.
