Booth renters and beauty pros

Booth renter taxes →
  • Do salon booth renters pay self-employment tax?Yes. A booth renter runs their own business, so the profit goes on Schedule C and self-employment tax of 15.3% applies once net earnings are $400 or more.
  • Is booth rent tax deductible?Yes. If you rent a chair, booth or suite to run your own beauty business, the rent is a business expense you deduct on Schedule C.
  • Do hairstylists qualify for no tax on tips?Yes. Barbers, hairstylists, cosmetologists, nail techs, lash and brow techs and makeup artists are on the IRS tipped occupations list.
  • When are 2026 estimated tax payments due?The four 2026 federal estimated tax payments are due April 15, June 15 and September 15, 2026, and January 15, 2027.

Landlords and real estate investors

Rental property taxes →
  • How many years do you depreciate a rental property?27.5 years for a residential rental building, using the mid-month convention.
  • When does depreciation start on a rental property?When the property is placed in service, meaning ready and available to rent, not when the first tenant moves in.
  • Is a new roof on a rental a repair or improvement?A new roof is an improvement. You add it to the property's basis and depreciate it over 27.5 years.
  • Can a rental property loss offset my W-2 income?Up to $25,000 a year, if you actively participate in managing the rental.
  • Is a security deposit taxable rental income?Not when you get it, if you may have to return it. It becomes income in the year you keep any of it, for example for damage or unpaid rent.
