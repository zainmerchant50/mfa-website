Federal Scholarship Tax Credit 2027: The New $1,700 Credit Explained

Starting Jan. 1, 2027, a cash gift to an approved scholarship organization can cut your federal tax dollar for dollar, up to $1,700. Here's how the new credit works, which states are in, and what it means if you live in New Jersey.

Executive summary

1
What it is: The federal scholarship tax credit (also called the Education Freedom Tax Credit, Internal Revenue Code section 25F) gives you a credit for cash donations to an approved scholarship granting organization (SGO) that funds K-12 scholarships.
2
How much: Up to $1,700 per person per year, or $3,400 for a married couple filing jointly. It's a nonrefundable credit, so it reduces tax you owe dollar for dollar.
3
When: It starts Jan. 1, 2027. Gifts made in 2026 don't count; 2027 gifts go on the 2027 return you file in 2028.
4
New Jersey: New Jersey was not on the IRS list of 30 participating states as of Sept. 14, 2026, but the IRS says you can give to an SGO in any participating state, wherever you live.

Bottom line: The federal scholarship tax credit 2027 start date matters. If you plan to give to K-12 scholarships, gifts made in 2027 can come back as a dollar-for-dollar credit of up to $1,700 per person.
