How to Have Your LLC Taxed as an S Corp: Form 2553 Guide

An LLC can choose S corporation tax treatment by filing Form 2553. Here's who qualifies, the deadline for 2027, what changes (payroll, a Form 1120-S) and when it pays off.

Hi there,

Thinking about having your LLC taxed as an S corp for 2027? We've put together a plain-English guide: who qualifies, the March 15, 2027 Form 2553 deadline, the reasonable-pay rule, the added costs and the New Jersey and New York steps.

Executive summary

1
How: an LLC gets S corporation tax treatment by filing Form 2553. You don't need to form a new company.
2
Deadline for 2027: file by March 15, 2027 to start January 1, 2027. A calendar-year company can also file at any time during 2026.
3
Who qualifies: 100 or fewer shareholders, all individuals, certain trusts or estates, no nonresident alien shareholders, and one class of stock.
4
What changes: owners who work in the business are paid reasonable wages through payroll, and the company files Form 1120-S plus a state return each year.

Bottom line: The election can lower self-employment tax once profit is comfortably above a reasonable salary. It also adds payroll and filing costs, so run the numbers before you file.
