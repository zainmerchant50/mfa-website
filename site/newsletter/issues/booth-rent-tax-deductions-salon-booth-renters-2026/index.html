Booth Rent and Taxes: What Salon Booth Renters Can Deduct (2026)

Rent a chair, booth or suite? The IRS treats you as a business owner. Here's what you can deduct on Schedule C for 2026, how much self-employment tax to plan for, and the dates that matter.

Hi there,

If you rent a chair or a suite, nobody withholds tax from your money, so every deduction you track is money you keep. We've put the booth renter write-offs, the 2026 numbers and the estimate dates on one page, with a checklist you can save before tax season.

Executive summary

1
Booth rent is tax deductible when you rent a chair, booth or suite to run your own beauty business. It goes on Schedule C.
2
Supplies, tools, licenses, education, advertising and insurance are deductible too. Tools up to $2,500 per item or invoice can be deducted at once under the de minimis safe harbor.
3
Self-employment tax is 15.3% once net earnings reach $400. Deductions lower the profit it's figured on.
4
2026 estimates are due April 15, June 15 and September 15, 2026, and January 15, 2027.

Bottom line: Pull a year of booth rent, product and app records now, and pay any 2026 expenses by December 31.
