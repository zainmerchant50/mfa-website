What we do

Bookkeeping, accounting and tax preparation under one roof

Most small businesses hire a bookkeeper and a tax preparer separately, then pay for the gaps between them. We handle both, so your books are ready when your return is due.

Bookkeeping

  • Monthly bookkeeping and bank/credit card reconciliations
  • Catch-up bookkeeping if you're months (or years) behind
  • QuickBooks Online setup and cleanup (QuickBooks ProAdvisor)
  • Payables, receivables and sales-tax tracking

Accounting

  • Month-end close with P&L, balance sheet and cash flow
  • Accrual-basis books for lenders, investors and buyers
  • Budgets, cash-flow forecasts and fractional CFO support
  • Year-end adjustments handed straight to your tax return

Tax preparation

  • Individual returns (1040), including self-employed and 1099 income
  • Business returns for LLCs, S corps, partnerships and C corps
  • New Jersey and multi-state returns
  • Extensions, prior-year and late filings, IRS notice help

Common questions

Before you reach out

Where are you located?

We're based in Union City, NJ and work with clients across Hudson County, including West New York, North Bergen, Weehawken, Hoboken, Jersey City, Guttenberg and Secaucus. Most work happens by video call, phone and secure document upload, so you don't need to take time off to drop off paperwork.

How much do you charge?

Most engagements are fixed-fee, quoted upfront after a free consultation, so you know exactly what you'll pay before we begin. Ongoing bookkeeping is a flat monthly fee based on your transaction volume.

I'm behind on my books or haven't filed. Can you help?

Yes. Catch-up bookkeeping and prior-year returns are a regular part of our work. We rebuild the records, reconcile every account and get your filings current.

Do you work with QuickBooks?

Yes. We're a QuickBooks ProAdvisor and handle QuickBooks Online setup, cleanup and ongoing bookkeeping. We can also work from spreadsheets and bank statements if you don't use accounting software yet.

What should I bring to the first call?

Nothing is required. If it's handy, have last year's tax return or a recent bank statement nearby so we can give you a more precise quote.
