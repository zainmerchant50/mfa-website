What we do

Bookkeeping, accounting and tax preparation under one roof

Most small businesses hire a bookkeeper and a tax preparer separately, then pay for the gaps between them. We handle both, so your books are ready when your return is due.

Bookkeeping

  • Monthly bookkeeping and bank/credit card reconciliations
  • Catch-up bookkeeping if you're months (or years) behind
  • QuickBooks Online setup and cleanup (QuickBooks ProAdvisor)
  • Payables, receivables and sales-tax tracking

Accounting

  • Month-end close with P&L, balance sheet and cash flow
  • Accrual-basis books for lenders, investors and buyers
  • Budgets, cash-flow forecasts and fractional CFO support
  • Year-end adjustments handed straight to your tax return

Tax preparation

  • Individual returns (1040), including self-employed and 1099 income
  • Business returns for LLCs, S corps, partnerships and C corps
  • New Jersey and multi-state returns
  • Extensions, prior-year and late filings, IRS notice help

We're based in Princeton, NJ and work with clients across Hudson County, including Union City, West New York, North Bergen, Weehawken, Hoboken, Jersey City, Guttenberg and Secaucus. Most work happens by video call, phone and secure document upload, so you don't need to take time off to drop off paperwork.

A senior team of experienced accountants and tax professionals, led by founder Zain Merchant, a member of ACCA (the Association of Chartered Certified Accountants, UK) with Big Four audit and assurance experience. For engagements that require a licensed CPA, we partner with licensed CPAs. You get one point of contact who knows your file.

You choose how you pay. Ongoing bookkeeping and advisory are usually a flat monthly fee, so your cost is predictable. One-off projects like cleanups and catch-up work get a project quote, and we also work on hourly contracts if you prefer to pay as you go. Bookkeeping pricing depends mainly on how many transactions you have each month, the number of accounts, the complexity of your business, and whether your books are kept on a cash or accrual basis.
