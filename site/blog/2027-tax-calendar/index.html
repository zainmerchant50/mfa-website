2027 Tax Calendar: Key IRS, New Jersey and New York Tax Deadlines

April 15, 2027 is the big one, but it's far from the only date that matters. Here is every key federal, New Jersey and New York tax deadline for 2027 in one place, with a calendar you can add to your phone.

By Zain Merchant, ACCAOctober 12, 20266 min read
SharePost on LinkedIn

Executive summary

  • Thursday, April 15, 2027 is Tax Day for 2026 individual returns (Form 1040, NJ-1040 and NY IT-201), C corporation returns and the first 2027 estimated payment.
  • Feb. 1, 2027 is the W-2 and 1099-NEC deadline. March 15, 2027 is the deadline for partnership and S corporation returns.
  • 2027 estimated tax due dates: April 15, June 15, Sept. 15 and Jan. 18, 2028. New Jersey and New York use the same dates.
  • Extended returns are due Oct. 15, 2027. Add every date below to your calendar in one click.

Bottom line: Put the 2027 dates that apply to you on your calendar now, especially April 15 and the four estimated tax payments, so nothing sneaks up on you.

This 2027 tax calendar lists the federal, New Jersey and New York deadlines most individuals and small businesses need, from the first estimated payment in January to extended returns in October. It covers calendar-year filers. If a date falls on a weekend or legal holiday, the deadline moves to the next business day, and the dates below already reflect that.

Important tax dates and deadlines in 2027 (federal)

DateTax deadline
Fri, Jan. 15, 2027Fourth-quarter 2026 estimated tax payment due (individuals; farmers and fishermen).
Mon, Feb. 1, 2027Give employees 2026 Forms W-2 and file W-2/W-3 with the Social Security Administration. Give recipients most 2026 Forms 1099 and file Form 1099-NEC with the IRS. File Form 941 for Q4 2026, Form 940 and Forms 943, 944 and 945 (Feb. 10 if all tax was deposited on time).

Mon, March 1, 2027File paper Forms 1099 with the IRS (with Form 1096). Farmers and fishermen who didn't pay a Jan. 15 estimate file their 2026 return and pay.

Wed, March 31, 2027Electronic filing deadline for most information returns (Forms 1097, 1098, 1099 except 1099-NEC, 3921, 3922 and W-2G).
Thu, April 15, 2027Tax Day. File 2026 Form 1040 or 1040-SR and pay (Form 4868 extends filing to Oct. 15, not payment). C corporations file Form 1120. First 2027 estimated tax payment due for individuals and corporations. Household employers file Schedule H.

Tue, June 15, 2027Second 2027 estimated tax payment. U.S. citizens and residents living abroad file Form 1040.
Mon, Aug. 2, 2027Form 941 for Q2 2027 (Aug. 10 if all tax was deposited on time).
Wed, Sept. 15, 2027Third 2027 estimated tax payment. Extended Forms 1065 and 1120-S due, with final K-1s.
Fri, Oct. 15, 2027Extended 2026 Form 1040 and Form 1120 due.

Note: Business deadlines above are for calendar-year entities with a Dec. 31 year-end; fiscal-year filers have different dates. The federal dates come from the IRS's early-release draft of Publication 509 for 2027, which can change before it's final. If a disaster declaration covers your county, the IRS and the states often postpone deadlines, so check the IRS disaster relief page.

How to use this 2027 tax calendar

  1. Add the dates to your calendar. Use the buttons below or subscribe to the MFA key-dates calendar, which updates itself when dates change.
  2. Mark the ones that apply to you. An employee with no side income may only need April 15. A business owner with employees and sales tax may have a deadline almost every month.
  3. Pay estimates on time. Missing an estimated payment can trigger an underpayment penalty even if you pay in full by April 15.
  4. Get your documents by mid-February. Most W-2s and 1099s arrive by Feb. 1 and brokerage 1099-Bs by Feb. 16, so start your return after that.
