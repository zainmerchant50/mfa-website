Wisconsin LLC

Forming an LLC in Wisconsin: fees, rules and timing

The state's own numbers, checked October 20, 2026, and what your first-year total looks like with MFA.

Wisconsin LLC at a glance

Formation documentArticles of Organization (Form 502)
Filing officeWisconsin Department of Financial Institutions (DFI)
State filing fee$130
Name reservation (optional)—
Annual report$25; due during the calendar quarter of the LLC's registration anniversary: March 31, June 30, September 30 or December 31. Wisconsin LLC annual report: $25 online, $40 on paper.
Also knowOnline formation costs $40 less than paper ($130 vs $170); annual report $15 less online ($25 vs $40)
Name must include 'limited liability company', 'limited company' or an abbreviation such as LLC
State tax registrationBusiness Tax Registration (BTR) with the Department of Revenue for seller's permits, employer withholding and similar accounts; initial BTR fee is $20 (covers two years), renewal $10
Registered agentRequired: a registered agent at a registered office in Wisconsin; the agent must be a Wisconsin resident, the LLC cannot be its own agent, and the office must be an actual street address (no mailbox, P.O. box or answering service)
