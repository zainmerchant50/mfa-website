Kansas LLC

Forming an LLC in Kansas: fees, rules and timing

The state's own numbers, checked October 20, 2026, and what your first-year total looks like with MFA.

Kansas LLC at a glance

Formation documentArticles of Organization
Filing officeKansas Secretary of State, Business Services Division
State filing fee$85
Name reservation (optional)—
Annual report$90; due April 15 of every other year: LLCs formed in an even year file in each following even year, and odd-year LLCs in each following odd year. Kansas calls it the Information Report and it is filed every two years. $90 online, $110 by paper (form revised 1/23/26). The statute sets $80 plus amounts in SOS rules (K.S.A. 17-76,139).
Also knowKansas LLCs file an Information Report every two years (April 15), not every year.
Missing the report leads to a three-month delinquency period and then forfeiture.
Kansas lowered some business fees in 2026; the formation and report amounts above come from forms revised in early 2026.
State tax registrationRegister with the Kansas Department of Revenue through the Customer Service Center questionnaire or Form CR-16 (Business Tax Application). No fixed deadline is published
Registered agentRequired: a resident agent that is an individual, a business registered in Kansas, or the LLC itself
