Idaho LLC

Forming an LLC in Idaho: fees, rules and timing

The state's own numbers, checked October 20, 2026, and what your first-year total looks like with MFA.

Idaho LLC at a glance

Formation documentCertificate of Organization
Filing officeIdaho Secretary of State, Business Services (filed through SOSBiz)
State filing fee$100
Name reservation (optional)$20
Annual reportSee the state's official page in the sources below; we confirm current state fees before invoicing.
Also knowPaper filings carry a $20 manual processing fee; online filing avoids it.
Annual report is due by the last day of the LLC's anniversary month, every year.
State tax registrationIdaho business registration (IBR) through the State Tax Commission for a seller's permit (sales/use tax) and, if you have employees, a withholding account. Register the business with the State of Idaho first. No fixed deadline is published
Registered agentRequired: an Idaho-resident individual or a business entity registered with the Secretary of State, with a physical Idaho street address (no PO boxes or private mailboxes)
Processing timeA banner on the SOS FAQ page gives this as a recent estimate; the state says processing times vary with filing volume.
