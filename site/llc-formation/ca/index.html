California LLC

Forming an LLC in California: fees, rules and timing

The state's own numbers, checked October 20, 2026, and what your first-year total looks like with MFA.

California LLC at a glance

Formation documentArticles of Organization (Form LLC-1)
Filing officeCalifornia Secretary of State, Business Programs Division
State filing fee$70
Name reservation (optional)$10
Annual report$20 (biennial); due every two years, in the month of registration (even or odd years depending on the registration year); SOS sends a reminder about three months before; missed filings get a delinquency notice and 60 more days. California calls this the Statement of Information (LLC-12), $20.00; the SOS FAQ says the fee is "$20 or $25". Separate from the $800 annual franchise tax paid to the Franchise Tax Board.
Also know$800 annual LLC franchise tax (Franchise Tax Board), due the 15th day of the 4th month after the SOS filing date, then annually (Form FTB 3522).
First-year $800 exemption applied only to tax years beginning 2021 through 2023 per the FTB page.
Additional LLC fee based on California income over $250,000: $900 ($250,000-$499,999), $2,500 ($500,000-$999,999), $6,000 ($1,000,000-$4,999,999), $11,790 ($5,000,000+); estimate due the 15th day of the 6th month (Form FTB 3536).
$250 penalty for not filing the Statement of Information.
Registered agentRequired: an agent for service of process who is a California-resident individual or a registered corporate agent; the LLC cannot be its own agent
Processing timeThe SOS updates its Current Processing Dates page; the figure shown is the published review date, not a guaranteed turnaround.
