    After you form

    Your first year, in order.

    What comes due after the LLC exists. Our packages cover the setup; this keeps you on track.

    1. Right after filing

      LLC filed, EIN issued, operating agreement signed. Open your business bank account.

    2. Within 60 days (NJ)

      File NJ-REG for state tax registration, sooner if you'll collect sales tax. New York has no general registration, but sales tax sellers register at least 20 days before starting.

    3. Within 120 days (NY)

      Finish the publication requirement and file the Certificate of Publication.

    4. If an S corp fits

      File Form 2553 on time and set up payroll for a reasonable salary.

    5. Each January

      Send 1099s to contractors you paid last year. Our 1099 checker tells you who needs one.

    6. Each anniversary month

      New Jersey annual report ($75). New York biennial statement every two years ($9).
