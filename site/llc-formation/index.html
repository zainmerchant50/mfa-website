    After you form

    Your first year, in order.

    What comes due after the LLC exists. Our packages cover the setup; this keeps you on track.

    1. Right after filing

      LLC filed, EIN issued, operating agreement signed. Open your business bank account.

    2. In the first weeks

      Register with your state's tax agency if your state or business needs it, for example NJ-REG in New Jersey within 60 days, and a sales tax permit before your first taxable sale.

    3. If your state requires it

      Finish any publication requirement (New York: within 120 days) and file the proof with the state.

    4. If an S corp fits

      File Form 2553 on time and set up payroll for a reasonable salary.

    5. Each January

      Send 1099s to contractors you paid last year. Our 1099 checker tells you who needs one.

    6. Every year (or two)

      Your state's annual or biennial report and any franchise tax. Your state's page shows the fee and due date.
