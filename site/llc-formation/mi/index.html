Michigan LLC

Forming an LLC in Michigan: fees, rules and timing

The state's own numbers, checked October 20, 2026, and what your first-year total looks like with MFA.

Michigan LLC at a glance

Formation documentArticles of Organization (Form CSCL/CD-700)
Filing officeMichigan Department of Licensing and Regulatory Affairs (LARA), Corporations Division
State filing fee$50
Name reservation (optional)—
Annual report$25; due February 15 each year, starting the year after formation (an LLC formed after September 30 does not file the following February 15). Michigan calls it the Annual Statement. Professional LLCs pay $75.
Also knowAnnual Statement is $25 and due every February 15 (first one the year after formation).
Online filings are paid by credit card only.
State tax registrationRegister through Michigan Treasury Online (MTO) for a sales tax license and, with employees, withholding. The state says you do not have to wait for the license to arrive before selling at retail
Registered agentRequired: a resident agent at a registered office with a Michigan street address
Processing timeThe state does not publish a standard processing time on its FAQ or form instructions.
