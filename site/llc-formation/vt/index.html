Vermont LLC

Forming an LLC in Vermont: fees, rules and timing

The state's own numbers, checked October 20, 2026, and what your first-year total looks like with MFA.

Vermont LLC at a glance

Formation documentArticles of Organization
Filing officeVermont Secretary of State, Business Services Division
State filing fee$155
Name reservation (optional)$25
Annual report$45; due within three months after the end of the LLC's fiscal year on record. Vermont LLC annual report. LLCs file annually to maintain legal existence.
Also knowAnnual report required every year; fee $45
Online filing is the state's preferred method with no extra fee
Registered agentRequired: the LLC must designate and continuously maintain a designated office (it need not be in Vermont) and an agent for service of process under 11 V.S.A. § 1655
Processing timeMailed filings take 7 to 10 business days.
