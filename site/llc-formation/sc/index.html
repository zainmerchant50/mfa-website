South Carolina LLC

Forming an LLC in South Carolina: fees, rules and timing

The state's own numbers, checked October 20, 2026, and what your first-year total looks like with MFA.

South Carolina LLC at a glance

Formation documentArticles of Organization
Filing officeSouth Carolina Secretary of State (Business Entities Online)
State filing fee$110
Name reservation (optional)—
Annual reportSouth Carolina has no Secretary of State annual report for an LLC. SCDOR says an LLC not taxed as a corporation is not subject to the annual report or License Fee. An LLC taxed as a corporation files a CL-1 annual report with SCDOR (License Fee: 0.1% of capital stock and paid-in surplus plus $15, minimum $25).
Also knowNo annual report for an LLC not taxed as a corporation
LLC taxed as a corporation files SC1120 or SC1120S and a CL-1 annual report with SCDOR
Business Personal Property account may be needed depending on location and NAICS code
State tax registrationRetail License ($50 non-refundable fee) before making taxable retail sales; Withholding account if you have employees working in SC; apply through the MyDORWAY Business Tax Application
Registered agentRequired: the articles must name an initial agent for service of process, who signs the form, with a street address in South Carolina
Processing timeThe state says online filing provides faster processing but does not publish a processing time.
