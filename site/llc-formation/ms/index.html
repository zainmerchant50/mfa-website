Mississippi LLC

Forming an LLC in Mississippi: fees, rules and timing

The state's own numbers, checked October 20, 2026, and what your first-year total looks like with MFA.

Mississippi LLC at a glance

Formation documentCertificate of Formation
Filing officeMississippi Secretary of State, Business Services
State filing fee$50
Name reservation (optional)$25
Annual report$0; due April 15 each year (filing opens January 1); must be filed online. Mississippi domestic LLCs file an annual report at no cost (fee schedule: 'Annual Report of MS LLC ... No fee'; SOS notice: domestic LLC owners can file annual reports 'at no cost'). A foreign LLC pays $250.
Also knowNo annual report fee for a domestic LLC, but the annual report is still required every year by April 15.
An operating agreement is recommended but not required by law (SOS startup guide).
State tax registrationMost entity types must register with the Mississippi Department of Revenue; registration is available online at www.DOR.ms.gov
Registered agentRequired: any individual, corporation or LLC may serve, provided the agent has a physical address in Mississippi; a non-commercial agent must consent before being listed
Processing timeNo guaranteed processing time or expedite fee is published for LLC formation.
