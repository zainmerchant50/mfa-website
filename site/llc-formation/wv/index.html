West Virginia LLC

Forming an LLC in West Virginia: fees, rules and timing

The state's own numbers, checked October 20, 2026, and what your first-year total looks like with MFA.

West Virginia LLC at a glance

Formation documentArticles of Organization of Limited Liability Company (Form LLD-1)
Filing officeWest Virginia Secretary of State (filed through the One Stop Business Portal)
State filing fee$101
Name reservation (optional)$15
Annual report$26; due every year between January 1 and June 30, starting the year after the year the LLC was registered. West Virginia annual report: $25 plus the $1.00 online processing fee. Waived for veteran-owned entities for four years after formation (Form LLD-1).
Also knowSeparate WV State Tax Department business registration ($30) in addition to Secretary of State filing
Online filings carry a $1.00 processing fee
Veteran-owned entities: registration fee waived and annual report fee waived for four years
State tax registrationBusiness registration with the WV State Tax Department is required before commencing business activities, after Secretary of State approval; $30 business registration tax, and the Business Registration Certificate does not expire
Registered agentOptional on the Articles ('if any'): the form says you may maintain an agent for service of process in West Virginia, who may be an individual resident, a corporation or another LLC
