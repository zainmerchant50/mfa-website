Arizona LLC

Forming an LLC in Arizona: fees, rules and timing

The state's own numbers, checked October 20, 2026, and what your first-year total looks like with MFA.

Arizona LLC at a glance

Formation documentArticles of Organization (Form L010)
Filing officeArizona Corporation Commission, Corporations Division
State filing fee$85
Name reservation (optional)$10
Annual report$0; due none: LLCs are not required to file annual reports. ACC FAQ: LLCs are not required to file annual reports (only corporations must).
PublicationDo not publish until the Commission approves the Articles. The instructions do not state a deadline. The ACC FAQ says filing the affidavit of publication is optional, but keep it with business records.
Also knowPublication of the filing is required; Maricopa and Pima County filings are posted on the ACC website automatically, other counties require newspaper publication.
No annual report for LLCs.
Transaction Privilege Tax (a form of sales/gross receipts tax) license, $12 per location, plus separate city licenses.
State tax registrationTransaction Privilege Tax (TPT) license from the Arizona Department of Revenue if selling products or services subject to TPT: $12 per location, via AZTaxes.gov or Joint Tax Application JT-1; register as soon as operations begin in Arizona
Registered agentRequired: a statutory agent, either a permanent full-time Arizona resident individual with an Arizona street address or an entity authorized in Arizona; the LLC cannot be its own statutory agent; the agent must accept in writing (Form M002)
Processing timeThe ACC posts updated processing times weekly at ecorp.azcc.gov.
