Forming an LLC in North Dakota: fees, rules and timing

The state's own numbers, checked October 20, 2026, and what your first-year total looks like with MFA.

North Dakota LLC at a glance

Formation documentArticles of Organization
Filing officeNorth Dakota Secretary of State, Business Services (FirstStop Portal)
State filing fee$135
Name reservation (optional)—
Annual report$50; due November 15 each year; reports must be received on or before the deadline
Also knowAnnual report is due November 15 and is filed through the FirstStop Portal.
Registered agentRequired: a North Dakota registered agent with a physical address in North Dakota. May be a North Dakota resident individual, or a corporation or LLC registered with the Secretary of State with a North Dakota office; the LLC cannot be its own agent
