Illinois LLC

Forming an LLC in Illinois: fees, rules and timing

The state's own numbers, checked October 20, 2026, and what your first-year total looks like with MFA.

Illinois LLC at a glance

Formation documentArticles of Organization (Form LLC-5.5)
Filing officeIllinois Secretary of State, Department of Business Services
State filing fee$150
Name reservation (optional)$25
Annual report$75; due filed within the 60 days immediately preceding the first day of the LLC's anniversary month, every year. LLC Annual Report (Form LLC-50.1). The statute adds $50 per series with a certificate of designation and a penalty if delinquent (805 ILCS 180/50-10).
Also knowAnnual report is $75 and is due in the 60 days before the first day of the anniversary month.
A payment processor fee is added to online card payments.
An LLC that can establish series pays $400 to form and $50 per series on the annual report.
State tax registrationRegister with the Illinois Department of Revenue (Form REG-1 / MyTax Illinois) before making any purchases or sales or hiring an employee. No fixed day count is published
Registered agentRequired: an individual who lives in Illinois or an entity authorized to transact business in Illinois; the registered office must be in Illinois with a street or rural route address (a PO box alone is not acceptable)
Processing timeThe 24-hour service total is $250.
