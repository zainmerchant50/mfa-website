Minnesota LLC

Forming an LLC in Minnesota: fees, rules and timing

The state's own numbers, checked October 20, 2026, and what your first-year total looks like with MFA.

Minnesota LLC at a glance

Formation documentArticles of Organization
Filing officeMinnesota Secretary of State, Business Services
State filing fee$155
Name reservation (optional)$55
Annual report$0; due December 31 each year (can be filed any time during the calendar year; the exact date appears on the Business Records Details page). Minnesota calls it the Annual Renewal. There is no fee for an active entity in good standing; reinstatement after dissolution is $85 online ($65 by mail).
Also knowNo state fee for the annual renewal; it is due December 31.
Online and in-person filings cost $20 more than mail ($155 vs $135).
A Minnesota registered office address is required; naming a registered agent is optional.
State tax registrationRegister with the Minnesota Department of Revenue for a Minnesota Tax ID Number and a Sales and Use Tax account before you make any taxable sales in Minnesota
Registered agentA Minnesota registered office address is required (street address or rural route; a PO box alone is not acceptable). Naming a registered agent is optional, but an agent named must be located at the registered office
Processing timeThe state says eligible online filings are expedited by default. In-person filings (by appointment) are processed while you wait; mail is first-come, first-served and not expedited.
