About Zain

Zain Merchant is an ACCA-qualified accountant and the founder of Merchant Financial Advisory (MFA). He has over 10 years of accounting, audit and tax experience. Before starting MFA, he worked at PwC in New York on audits, SOX and internal-controls engagements for Fortune 500 financial services clients, with a rotation through tax, and spent a short period at EY.

Today he helps individuals, freelancers and small and mid-sized businesses keep accurate, accrual-basis books, stay on top of IRS and New Jersey filing deadlines, and get more out of QuickBooks. He is a certified QuickBooks ProAdvisor.
