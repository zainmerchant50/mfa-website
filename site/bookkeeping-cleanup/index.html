Bookkeeping cleanup

Behind on your books? We'll get you caught up.

Cleanup depends on how many months, accounts and transactions are involved, so we don't guess at a flat rate. Send a few details, and we'll give you a fixed quote before any work starts.

What cleanup covers

From a pile of transactions to books you can use.

Catch-up categorization

Every transaction since the point you fell behind sorted to a chart of accounts that fits your business.

Bank and card reconciliations

Each bank and credit card account reconciled to its statements, month by month, with differences explained.

Payables and receivables tidy-up

Unpaid bills and open invoices matched to payments, duplicates and stale items cleared out.

Opening balances

Starting balances set correctly, so the books agree with where the accounts stand today.

A short written summary

A plain-English note of what we found and fixed, and what to do differently going forward.
